# A template may carry text, a logo, or a badge

ADR 0001 forbade text, logos, and badges on a template so every grid would be safe to upload under Steamworks' library-capsule rules. That exclusion is reversed. The templates for this phase are platform chrome, and they ship with their lettering, marks, and badges. A grid made from one is for local use. It is not promised to pass Steamworks review.
